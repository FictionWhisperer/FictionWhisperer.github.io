Common Quest Issues

Stuck on a quest? Check below before asking in chat. Click a question to reveal the answer.

Overworld Quests

Place the leather armor and blue dye together in your crafting table. It will look unpainted inside the grid, but once you take it out it will be dyed correctly.
You can buy carved pumpkins directly from the shop for 1,000 coins — no crafting required.
You have to manually place cocoa beans on your jungle logs/trees first. Once placed, they'll grow and you can harvest more.
Taming ocelots is slightly different on CubeCraft. Watch: How to Tame Cats on CubeCraft Skyblock
Go to the Hub and talk to the Optimiser NPC.

Nether Quests

Almost all Nether quests must be completed in the Nether itself. Make sure you're in the Nether biome when making the kills. The only exceptions are gathering Lapis Lazuli and Quartz.
Use any tool with Fortune on it to speed up collection significantly. You can also build the roots 2 blocks lower than your platform level — it's easier to reach upward and mine the glowstone that way.
Looting significantly increases gold ingot drops from piglins. Also, dropping piglins a few blocks before killing them lets you one-shot them faster and cycle through kills more quickly.
Magma Slimes have an unusual hitbox. Aim for the bottom-middle of the mob to register hits reliably.
Build a box of cobblestone generators around the spawn point — aim for around 3 blocks thick. Before spawning the Wither, surround the space where its head will be with 4 cobblestone generators. This creates a self-repairing cage it can't blow through. Use a sword with the highest Smite level you can manage.
