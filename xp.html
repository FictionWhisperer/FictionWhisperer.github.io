⚗️ XP Farming

Levelling Up Fast

From starter ore grinding to high-output wither skeleton setups — Bedrock and Java both covered.

XP Progression Order

Ores → Piglins → Wither Skeletons

Before your first nether island is unlocked, grinding ores gets you to Level 5 stone tools — no mob farms needed yet. Unlocking the Nether is your next priority.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Grab Piglin Spawners from the first Nether island. These are your XP foundation for the early and mid game.
  2. Build a small dropper in the Nether to farm starter XP and complete quests simultaneously.
    Using fencegates lets mobs spawn directly into the drop chamber for faster kills.
    Video coming soon.
  3. Build a 127-block-high dropper. Make sure mobs spawn exactly 127 blocks above the player (drop will be ~125–126 blocks).
    Dropper Tutorial (YouTube)
  4. Upgrade Piglin Spawners to Level 95. At this level they survive the fall and can be one-hit, making them extremely efficient XP.
  5. Craft a high Smite sword. As high as possible — you'll need it for XP farming and eventually for the Wither boss fight.
  6. Unlock Wither Skeletons from the 3rd Nether island. Add them to your dropper alongside Piglins and upgrade them to the same spawner level.
  7. Late game: Once you have significant money, buy more Wither Skeleton spawners and phase out the Piglins entirely for maximum XP output.

Key Tips

Dropper Height

127 blocks above player = mobs survive the fall at spawner level 95. Don't go higher or they'll take too little fall damage.

Smite Sword

Max Smite is essential — Piglins and Wither Skeletons are undead mobs. Smite 20 is ideal; Smite 15 + Unbreaking 5 is a solid XP build.

Wither Boss

Spawn the Wither inside a ~3-block-thick cobblestone box. Surround where its head will be with 4 cobble generators before spawning.

Fencegates

Mobs can spawn on fencegates. Using them in the spawn volume means mobs land directly in the kill chamber — no dropper gap needed.
