✨ Enchantments

Best Enchants

Optimal enchantments for every tool and armor piece. Max enchant level on CubeCraft is 20.

Java Players: +2 Efficiency All Efficiency values below are for Bedrock. Java players add +2 to every Efficiency recommendation (e.g. Eff 6 → Eff 8).

Late Game — Tools

⛏️ Pickaxe General / Ores

  • Efficiency 6 (8 Java)
  • Fortune 11
  • Unbreaking 3

Fortune 14 if you prefer maximum drops over unbreaking.

⛏️ Pickaxe Silk Touch / Ice

  • Silk Touch 1
  • Efficiency 4 (ice) / 6 (other)
  • Unbreaking 15

🪓 Axe General Mining

  • Efficiency 5 (7 Java)
  • Fortune 11
  • Unbreaking 3

Build-only axe: Efficiency 5, Unbreaking 15.

🪣 Shovel

  • Efficiency 2
  • Unbreaking 18
  • Silk Touch 1 (if doing grass)

Only add Silk Touch if you also harvest grass.

🌾 Hoe

  • Fortune 20
  • or Unbreaking 20
  • or Eff 2 + Silk + Ub 17 (leaves)

Preference-based — pick what suits your playstyle.

Swords — By Purpose

⚔️ Wither Killer

  • Smite 20

⚔️ XP Farming

  • Smite 15
  • Unbreaking 5

Or Smite 20 if you prefer max damage.

⚔️ Non-Undead Mobs

  • Sharpness 15
  • Unbreaking 5
  • Looting optional

⚔️ Quests / Money / Drops

  • Looting 20
  • Smite or Sharp lower levels

Lower Smite/Sharp means slower kills but better drops.

Late Game — Armor

⚠️ Protection Cap Total protection is capped at 80%. Distribution doesn't matter — 20 levels on one piece gives the same protection as 5 levels on each piece. Don't stack above 20 total.
Useless on CubeCraft: Projectile Protection and Feather Falling — normal Protection maxes out so these have no benefit. Fire Protection and Blast Resistance are only useful for reduced burn time and explosion knockback.

🪖 Helmet

  • Aqua Affinity 1
  • Respiration 2
  • Blast Protection 7
  • Unbreaking 10

🧥 Chestplate

  • Protection 10
  • Unbreaking 10

👖 Leggings

  • Protection 10
  • Unbreaking 10

👟 Boots

  • Depth Strider 3
  • Fire Protection 7
  • Unbreaking 10

Early Game — Level 5 Stone Tools

⛏️ Pickaxe (Early)

  • General use: Efficiency 3–4
  • General use: Unbreaking 1–2
  • Ores/Quests: Fortune 5

Also make a Silk Touch pickaxe for limited resources like cobwebs.

🪓 Axe (Early)

  • Efficiency 3–4
  • Unbreaking 1–2

Unbreaking 2 = less repair. Efficiency 4 = faster mining.

🪣 Shovel (Early)

  • Efficiency 3
  • Unbreaking 2

🌾 Hoe (Early)

  • Unbreaking 5

Or whatever you prefer for your farm setup.

⚔️ Sword (Early)

  • Looting 5 (mob drop quests)
  • Smite 5 (small XP)

Ores are better XP than mobs before you unlock piglins.

🛡️ Early Armor (Iron)

  • Protection 2–3 per piece

No armor enchants are strictly needed at this stage — only enchant if your XP source allows it within reasonable time.
