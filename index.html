Quick Tips

Spawners

Fencegates maximize spawn output — mobs land directly in the kill chamber.

Fortune

Fortune 10 = guaranteed +1 drop. Fortune 15 = +1 guaranteed, 50% chance at +2.

Looting

Looting 4 = guaranteed +1 mob drop. Great for gold quests with piglins.

Protection

Protection caps at 80% total. Spreading 20 levels across any pieces achieves max — distribution doesn't matter.

Droppers

127 blocks high = mobs survive fall at level 95, can be one-hit. Perfect XP efficiency.

Java +2

Java players get +2 Efficiency on all tool recommendations vs Bedrock.
