How to Make Money

Fastest Progression Path

1
Cocoa Beans Only needed to complete the "Blot out the Sun" quest. Don't spend time here.
2
Melon Farm Build a large melon farm on Birch Island. Use Fortune + Silk Touch on an axe, or Efficiency + Silk Touch for a bigger farm. Aim to gather a few million before Savannah.
3
Pig Spawners × 864 When you hit Savannah, buy pig spawners and build the full fencegate pig farm. Complete all 864 spawners before moving on.
4
Creeper Farm (optional) Only worth it if you plan to grind 200+ hours manually. See the breakdown below before deciding.
⚠️ Also focus on unlocking the Nether in parallel — talk to the Gatekeeper to progress. Remove lava from farms and swap the chessboard fence/glass-pane pattern with cobblestone walls + lanterns.

How to Farm XP

Progression Order

Ores → Piglins → Wither Skeletons

Unlock the first Nether Island as your top priority if you haven't yet. You can reach level 5 stone tools from ores alone — no mob farm needed at first.

1
Grab Piglin Spawners Pick up the piglin spawners from the first Nether Island. These are your XP foundation.
2
Build a Small Dropper Set up a small dropper in the Nether to farm starter XP and complete quests simultaneously. With fencegates, mobs can spawn directly into the drop chamber for speed.
3
Build a 127-Block High Dropper Once further along, build a full-height dropper. Mobs must spawn exactly 127 blocks above the player (drop lands at ~125–126 blocks).
4
Upgrade Piglins to Level 95 At level 95, piglins survive the fall and are one-hit kills — clean, easy XP. Get a sword with the highest Smite level you can for this phase.
5
Add Wither Skeletons After unlocking the third Nether Island, grab wither skeleton spawners and combine them with your piglins. Upgrade them to match piglin level.
6
Late Game: Switch to Wither Skeletons When you have a lot of money, buy more wither skeleton spawners and retire the piglins entirely.
