⚙️ Game Mechanics

How Things Work

CubeCraft Skyblock changes several vanilla mechanics. Know them — they affect every farm and strategy you build.

Spawners

Spawn Volume

Spawners use the same 9×3×9 volume as vanilla — 4 blocks in each horizontal direction, 1 block up and down. You must be within 2 chunks for the spawner to activate.

Mobs Spawn on Any Block (not air)

Unlike vanilla, mobs on CubeCraft do not require air to spawn on. They will spawn on any block — including non-full blocks like slabs and fencegates. This is the key to efficient farm design.

Spawn Cycle & Capacity

Every 3 seconds, the spawner attempts to spawn. The more blocks available in the spawn volume, the higher the chance of a spawn. Spawners also have a max capacity — if too many mobs are alive from the previous cycle, fewer will spawn. Kill mobs before the next cycle for maximum output.

Max efficiency = all mobs killed before next 3s spawn cycle

Fencegates = Best Spawn Surface

Open fencegates give the most available spawn surface area in practice, and mobs can drop directly into a kill chamber. This is why fencegate farms are the meta.

⚠️ Spawner Levelling does NOT increase item drops Levelling up a spawner only increases XP drops, and the mob's health, damage, and armor. It does not improve loot. Keep this in mind when deciding where to invest your money.

Mob Drop Differences

Certain mobs on CubeCraft don't drop everything they would in vanilla Minecraft:

  • Zombies ❌ Do not drop Iron
  • Witches ❌ Do not drop Redstone

Custom Enchant Mechanics

Max Enchant Level: 20 CubeCraft uses custom enchants with modified vanilla logic. The cap for all enchants is level 20.

Fortune — How it Actually Works

Fortune on CubeCraft works on any timer-based generated resource: generators, crops, trees (not leaves). It applies as long as the tool can normally harvest the resource.

Example: A Fortune pickaxe works on crops. A Fortune axe does not work on coal ore (an axe doesn't normally drop coal).

Fortune = 10% chance per level to drop 1 extra item (not double)

Fortune 10 = 100% chance of +1 extra (guaranteed)
Fortune 15 = 100% chance of +1, 50% chance of +2

Looting — How it Works

Looting only works on mobs (not blocks). Each level gives a 25% chance for one extra drop.

Looting = 25% chance per level for 1 extra mob drop

Looting 4 = 100% chance of +1 drop (guaranteed)
Looting 8 = 100% chance of +1, plus 100% chance of +2 (guaranteed +2)

Armor Protection — How it Works

All protection enchants work the same as vanilla, but with the max level of 20, normal Protection dominates entirely.

  • Protection is capped at 80% total regardless of how many levels you have.
  • Distribution doesn't matter — Protection 20 on one piece = the same cap as 5 on each piece.
  • Projectile Protection and Feather Falling are useless since normal Protection is already maxed.
  • Fire Protection and Blast Resistance are only worth it for their side effects (reduced burn time / explosion knockback).
