01 / Money

Getting Started

Follow this path in order. Skipping steps will cost you more in the long run.

🫘
Cocoa Beans
Only farm these to complete the "Blot out the Sun" quest. Don't invest further — this is purely a quest gate.
Quest only
🍈
Melon Farm — Birch Island
Build a large melon farm on Birch Island. Use Fortune + Silk Touch on an axe for max drops, or go bigger and use Efficiency + Silk Touch to mine faster. Target a few million before hitting Savannah.
Good starter income
🔥
Unlock the Nether (parallel)
While farming melons, actively work on unlocking the Nether — talk to the Gatekeeper to progress. This opens XP farms and better spawners.
Do this in parallel
🐷
Pig Spawners × 864 — Full Build
At Savannah, buy pig spawners and build the full fencegate pig farm. Remove lava. Replace the chessboard fence/glass-pane pattern with cobblestone walls + lanterns.
Complete ALL 864 before moving on
💥
Creeper Farm (optional)
Only worth upgrading if you plan to sell for 200+ hours manually. Pigs give better value per coin spent. Finish pigs completely, then decide.
Only if 200h+ grinder

06 / XP

Bedrock & Java

Same progression order on both platforms — different endgame farms.

1

Grab Piglin Spawners

Collect piglin spawners from the first Nether Island — these are your XP foundation for the entire early game.

2

Build a Small Dropper

Set up a small dropper in the Nether for starter XP and quests at the same time. Use fencegates so mobs spawn directly into the drop chamber for speed.

3

127-Block High Dropper

Build a full-height dropper once you're further along. Mobs must spawn exactly 127 blocks above the player — not higher. Drop lands at ~125–126 blocks.

4

Upgrade Piglins to Level 95

At level 95, piglins survive the fall and are one-hit kills. Get a sword with the highest Smite you can — you'll need it for XP and eventually the Wither.

5

Add Wither Skeleton Spawners

After unlocking the third Nether Island, grab wither skeleton spawners and combine with your piglins. Upgrade them to the same level.

6

Late Game: Full Wither Skeletons

When you have a lot of money, buy more wither skeleton spawners and retire your piglins entirely — skeletons are the better endgame XP source.
